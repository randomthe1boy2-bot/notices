Frequently Asked Questions

Will my server data or logs be stored after October 1st?

No. Once the bot is turned off on October 1st, 2026, all cached configurations and temporary data will be securely and permanently deleted from our servers.

Do I need to kick the bot from my server manually?

You don't have to, but you can safely remove it from your Discord Server settings at any time. It will simply appear offline after October 1st.
